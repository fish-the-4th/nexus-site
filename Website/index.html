What Nexus does

The core systems your server will actually use, all built in from day one.

🛡️

Moderation

The backbone of any Discord server. Every infraction gets a case number, and members can be banned even outside the server if you don't want them joining. Nexus always checks the role hierarchy first, so lower roles can't act on higher ones.

kickbanmutewarnpurgecasesmyhistory
🚨

AutoMod

Works in the background. It catches message spam, blocks known scam links, and can lock the whole server down automatically if it detects a raid. Every type has its own sensitivity, adjustable from 1 to 10.

automod setupautomod raidendautomods
🔐

Autoroles

Join roles reserved for those who joined, persistent roles staying on them even if they leave and join back, and timed roles which gives a temporary role to a member until the timer stops.

joinrolesrole persisttimed role
🎫

Tickets

Members open a private ticket channel with a single click. Staff can bring people in or out, and close it once it's handled.

ticket setupticket panelticket close
📊

Stats

Find out who's actually active with message and voice activity over the last 30 days, as a graph, for one person, one channel, or the whole server. See who joined or left, and which invite brought them in.

stats userstats serverstats invite
📋

Logging

Nine separate logging categories, from messages and users to moderation actions and role changes, each routable to its own channel so nothing gets lost in one giant feed.

loglogslogging ignore
🎮

Games

Ten games are built right in and one command cam pull up the whole menu, and every game keeps your stats across servers. Games can be played with the bot or other members in the server.

gamesgames stats
