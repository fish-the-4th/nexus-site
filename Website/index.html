What it does

The parts most servers end up using. Every command is in the command list, and every one works as a slash command too.

Moderation

Every warn, mute, kick and ban gets a case number you can look up later. You can ban someone who isn't in the server yet, and Nexus checks the role order so nobody can act on someone above them.

warnmutebancasesmyhistory

AutoMod

Catches message spam and known scam links, and locks the server down if a raid starts. Each one has its own 1 to 10 setting, so you decide how strict it is.

automod setupautomod raidend

Logging

Nine kinds of logs, from deleted messages to role changes. Send each one to its own channel instead of one feed nobody reads.

loglogslogging ignore

Tickets

Members open a private channel with one button. Staff can add or remove people, and closed tickets are logged.

ticket setupticket panel

Roles

Give new members a role when they join, give back someone's roles if they leave and rejoin, or hand out a role that takes itself off later.

joinrolesrole persisttimed role

Stats

Messages and voice time over the last 30 days, as a graph, for a person, a channel or the whole server. It also shows which invite brought people in.

stats userstats serverstats invite

There's also a set of ten games (trivia, blackjack, hangman, Connect Four, 2048 and more), reminders, giveaways, a starboard, translation, private staff notes and a custom prefix.

Getting started

  1. Invite Nexus to a server you manage, then drag its role above the roles it should be able to give out.
  2. Type -commands (or /commands). It opens a menu with every command, grouped, that only you can see.
  3. Pick a log channel first with -log, then turn on AutoMod or tickets when you're ready. The getting started guide goes through it step by step.

Stuck on something? Ask in the support server, or check whether Nexus is up.
