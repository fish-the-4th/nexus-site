Something not working?

Most problems come down to a missing permission, the role order, or a small typo. Find your problem below. If you're still stuck, the support server is one click away.

Getting Nexus to respond

Nexus doesn't answer any command
  1. Run -ping in a different channel. If it answers there, the first channel is blocking Nexus.
  2. Check the channel's permissions: Nexus needs View Channel and Send Messages there, and Embed Links because it replies with embeds.
  3. Check the prefix. Mention Nexus with @Nexus prefix to see it, since a changed prefix applies to the whole server.
  4. Look at the status page in case Nexus is down.
I forgot the prefix

Mention Nexus instead: @Nexus prefix. Mentioning Nexus works as a prefix for every command, always.

Commands don't work in DMs

That's on purpose. In a DM only -ping, -commands and -help work. Everything else needs a server.

It says to slow down

Each person can run one command every 2 seconds, and the same command once every 3 seconds. Wait a moment and try again. It only affects you.

Permissions and roles

"I can't do that to X, their role sits above mine"

This is the most common problem. Discord only lets a bot act on people and roles below its own role.

  1. Open Server Settings → Roles.
  2. Drag the Nexus role up, above every role it needs to manage or act on.
  3. Keep it below your own admin roles.
"You do not have permission" when I run a command

The command needs a Discord permission you don't have. The command list shows what each one needs. A server admin can let your role use it anyway with command access, for example -permission warn @Helpers.

I can't ban, kick or mute someone

Either their top role is equal to or above yours, or above Nexus's. You can never act on someone above you, and Nexus can't act on anyone above its role. Server administrators and the owner can't be muted with a timeout at all, because Discord doesn't allow it.

A role can't be given out

The role is above Nexus's role, above your own top role, or it's managed by an integration (like a bot's own role or the booster role). Move the Nexus role up, or pick a different role.

Moderation

The member wasn't sent a DM

Their DMs are closed. The action still went through, and the case records it with the reason.

Purge deleted fewer messages than I asked

Discord won't let bots bulk delete messages older than 14 days, and Nexus looks through the most recent messages in the channel for that person's. Anything older stays.

I made a mistake with a ban or mute

Run -casedelete [case number]. It deletes the case and lifts the ban or mute it created. It needs Ban Members, and the moderation log keeps a record of who removed it.

AutoMod and logging

AutoMod isn't catching anything

Check -automods to see each type's level. A level of 0 means that type is off. Also check the person or one of their roles isn't on the whitelist, since whitelisted people are never checked.

AutoMod keeps deleting normal chat

Lower the spam level by one with -automod setup spam [level], and check the AutoMod log over a few days. If one trusted person keeps being caught, whitelist them instead of lowering the whole server's level.

Nothing shows up in my log channel

Run -logs to see where each type goes. Nexus needs to view the channel and send messages and embeds there. Messages from bots aren't logged, and deleted messages can only show their text if Nexus saw them while it was online.

Tickets, giveaways and more

The ticket button does nothing

Make sure -ticket setup was run, and that Nexus has Manage Channels and Manage Roles and can manage the ticket category.

My giveaway has no 🎉 reaction

Nexus needs Add Reactions in that channel. Fix the permission, then start the giveaway again.

A giveaway, reminder or timed role was a little late

Giveaways and reminders are checked every 30 seconds, and timed roles once a minute, so a short delay is normal.

Stats are empty

Stats start counting when Nexus joins your server. A stats command shows the last 30 days unless you add 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days on the end, and Nexus keeps 90 days at most. A brand-new server needs a few days before the graphs fill in.

The dashboard

I can't log in to the dashboard

Try again in a minute, since logins are rate limited. Make sure you're logging in with the same Discord account you use in the server, and that cookies aren't blocked for discord.com. If it says the login expired, start the login again from the dashboard page.

The admin dashboard is locked

Only the server owner and members with the Administrator permission can open it. Everyone else can still open their own member dashboard.
