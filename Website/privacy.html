Privacy

What Nexus keeps, how long it keeps it, and how to ask for it to be removed. Last updated 10 November 2026.

What Nexus keeps

  • Moderation cases. The person actioned, the action, the reason, the moderator and the time. Kept until a moderator deletes the case.
  • Activity counts. How many messages someone sent and how long they spent in voice, with user and channel IDs and timestamps. Removed after 30 days.
  • Join and leave records. Which invite was used and when someone joined or left. Removed after 30 days.
  • Server settings. Prefix, join roles, ticket setup, logging channels, AutoMod levels and command permissions. Kept while the server uses Nexus.

What Nexus doesn't keep

  • The text of your messages. Stats only count them.
  • The contents of direct messages sent to Nexus.
  • Anything about a server after Nexus has left it, beyond what's listed above.

Asking for a wipe

You can ask for your server's data to be wiped by posting in the support server. Requests are reviewed by hand. Wipes aren't guaranteed, but where one is possible, it removes the stored data for that server.

Changes to this page

If what Nexus stores changes, this page will change with it, and the date at the top will be updated.
