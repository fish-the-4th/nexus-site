About

An all-in-one bot, built on logic.

Nexus is one Discord bot that handles moderation, protection, logging, tickets, roles, stats, games and everyday tools, so your server doesn't need a different bot for every job.

What Nexus is

Nexus is an all-in-one Discord bot. It covers the systems a community actually runs on: moderation with numbered cases, AutoMod that stops spam, scam links and raids, logging for nine kinds of events, private tickets, automatic roles, 30-day activity stats, giveaways, a starboard and ten built-in games.

Everything is controlled from inside Discord with plain commands, and there's no account to create. Type -commands to see what you can use. If you'd rather click than type, the dashboard lets admins change settings and lets every member see their own stats and history.

Why it exists

Most communities end up with a pile of bots that each do one thing. Every one has its own prefix, its own permissions and its own premium plan, and there's a real chance they get in each other's way. Two bots logging the same event, or two AutoMods fighting over one message, is more common than it should be.

Nexus started with a simple idea: one bot that covers the essentials well, is easy to understand, and doesn't hide the basics behind a paywall.

How it works

Three ideas cover almost everything.

⌨️

Commands in Discord

Every feature is a text command with the server's prefix, - by default. Mentioning Nexus always works too.

🗂️

Settings per server

What you set up in one server never touches another. Cases, logs and stats stay with the server that made them.

🛡️

Safe by default

Every action checks your permission and the role order first, and leaves a record in cases and logs.
