About Nexus

A bot built to handle all of your server's needs. A complete, all-in-one bot.

What Nexus is

Nexus is an all-in-one Discord bot. Moderation, tickets, roles, logging, stats, and much more, so a server doesn't need a different bot for every job.

Everything is controlled from inside Discord with simple commands. There's no account to create and no dashboard to learn. Type -commands and you can see what's available.

Why it exists

Most communities end up with a pile of bots that each do one thing, each with their own prefix. That creates a lot of clutter, and there's a real chance the bots end up breaking each other. Nexus started with a simple idea: one bot that covers the essentials.

Our slogan says it best: Built on logic, built for everyone.

What Nexus stores

Nexus puts user safety and privacy first. We would like to keep no data at all, but some pieces have to be saved to keep all functions working:

  • Moderation cases. Who was actioned, what happened, the reason and when. These stay until a moderator deletes the case.
  • Activity stats. How many messages someone sent and how long they spent in voice, with user and channel IDs and timestamps. The text of your messages is not stored for stats. This data is removed automatically after 30 days.
  • Join and leave records. Which invite a member used and when people join or leave. These are also removed after 30 days.
  • Settings. The things your server configures, like prefixes, join roles, ticket setup, logging channels and AutoMod levels.
Questions about your data? Ask in the support server. You can also ask for your data to be wiped. Data wipes are not guaranteed, but it is sometimes possible to work something out.

Meet the staff

Nexus is built by a sole developer, and staff members look after moderation and support. You can see who they are on the staff page, and if something isn't working, the status page will tell you what's going on.
