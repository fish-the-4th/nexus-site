About Nexus

Nexus is one Discord bot for moderation, AutoMod, logging, tickets, roles, stats, games and everyday tools, so a server doesn't need a different bot for every job.

Why it exists

Most communities end up with a pile of bots that each do one thing. Every one has its own prefix, its own permissions and its own premium plan, and they get in each other's way more often than you'd think: two bots logging the same event, or two AutoMods fighting over one message.

Nexus started from a simple idea. One bot that covers the essentials properly, is easy to understand, and doesn't hide the basics behind a paywall.

What it does

Moderation with a case number for every action. AutoMod for spam, scam links and raids. Nine kinds of logs, each sent where you want. Private tickets, roles that are given out automatically, 30 days of activity stats, giveaways, a starboard and ten games. The command list has every command.

It all runs from Discord, with the prefix (- by default) or as slash commands. If you'd rather click than type, the dashboard lets admins change settings and lets anyone see their own stats and history.

What it's built around

  • One bot instead of several. One prefix, one set of permissions, one log setup. The features know about each other, so nothing fires twice.
  • Free. There's no premium tier. Everything on this site works in every server.
  • Not keeping more than it needs. Nexus counts activity but never stores what people say, and activity older than 30 days is deleted. The privacy page has the details.
  • Plain replies. When something works, it says what happened. When it can't, it says why and what to fix.

How it works

Settings belong to the server they were made in, so nothing you set up in one server touches another. Every moderation action checks your permissions and the role order first, then leaves a record in cases and logs.
