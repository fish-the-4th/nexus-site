What Nexus is

Nexus is an all-in-one Discord bot. It covers the systems that every server needs: moderation with numbered cases, automod that stops spam, scam links and raids, logging for nine kinds of events, private tickets, automatic roles, stats, giveaways, a starboard and twelve built-in games.

Everything can be controlled from inside Discord with plain command or on the website with the dashboard. Type -commands to see what you can use. If you'd rather click than type, the dashboard lets admins change settings and lets every member see their own stats and history.

Why it exists

Most communities end up with a pile of bots that each do one thing. Every one has its own prefix, its own permissions and its own premium plan, and they always conflict with each other. Two bots logging the same event, or two AutoMods fighting over one message, is more common than it should be.

Nexus started with one simple idea: A bot that can do all your servers tasks, no bots conflciting, one central hub, and all your basic functions comletely free.
