What Nexus is

Nexus is an all-in-one Discord bot. Moderation, tickets, roles, logging, stats, and much more, so a server doesn't need a different bot for every job.

Everything is controlled from inside Discord with simple commands, and there's no account to create. Type -commands to see what's available. If you'd rather click than type, the dashboard lets admins change settings and lets every member see their own stats and history.

Why it exists

Most communities end up with a pile of bots that each do one thing, each with their own prefix. That creates clutter, and there's a real chance the bots end up breaking each other.

Nexus started with a simple idea: one bot that covers the essentials.
