About Nexus

A bot built to handle the everyday work of running a server, and to do it properly.

What Nexus is

Nexus is an all-in-one Discord bot. Moderation, automatic protection, tickets, roles, logging, stats and a handful of games all live in the same place, so a server doesn't need a different bot for every job.

Everything is controlled from inside Discord with simple commands. There's no account to create and no dashboard to learn. Type -commands and you can see what's available.

Why it exists

Most communities end up with a pile of bots that each do one thing, each with their own prefix, their own quirks and their own way of breaking. Nexus started with a simple idea: one bot that covers the essentials well, and keeps working the same way everywhere.

The tagline says it best. Built on logic, built for everyone.

How it's meant to behave

Records you can trust

Every moderation action becomes a numbered case with the reason, the moderator and the time, so there's always something to point back to.

Fair to members

When someone is actioned, they get a DM that says what happened and why. No guessing.

Respects your hierarchy

Nexus checks roles before acting. Moderators can't use it to act on people above them, and it tells you plainly when it can't do something.

Quiet when it should be

It ignores unknown commands and doesn't spam channels. Protection runs in the background until it's needed.

What Nexus stores

Nexus only keeps what it needs to do its job. In plain terms:

  • Moderation cases. Who was actioned, what happened, the reason and when. These stay until a moderator deletes the case.
  • Activity stats. How many messages someone sent and how long they spent in voice, with user and channel IDs and timestamps. The text of your messages is not stored for stats. This data is removed automatically after 30 days.
  • Join and leave records. Which invite a member used and when people join or leave, also removed after 30 days.
  • Settings. The things your server configures, like prefixes, join roles, ticket setup, logging channels and AutoMod levels.
Questions about your data? Ask in the support server and you'll get a straight answer.
