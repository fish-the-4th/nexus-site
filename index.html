What Nexus does

These are the core systems your server will actually use, all built in from day one.

🛡️

Moderation

A case-based moderation system that keeps a real record of everything. Every action gets its own case number, and members get a DM explaining exactly what happened and why.

kickbanmutewarnpurgecasesvoid
🚨

AutoMod

Works quietly in the background. It catches message spam, blocks known scam links, and can lock the whole server down automatically if it detects a raid. Every type has its own sensitivity, adjustable from 1 to 10.

automod setupautomod raidendautomods
🔐

Autoroles

Give new members a role automatically the moment they join, keep someone's roles intact if they leave and come back, or hand out a role that quietly removes itself once time's up.

joinrolesrole persisttimed role
🎫

Tickets

Members open a private ticket channel with a single click. Staff can claim it, bring other people in or out, and close it once it's handled.

ticket setupticket panelticket close
📊

Stats

Find out who's actually active. Nexus tracks message and voice activity over the last 30 days and turns it into a graph, whether you're looking at one person, one channel, or the whole server's leaderboard.

stats userstats channelstats top
📋

Logging

Nine separate logging categories, from messages and users to moderation actions and role changes, each one routable to its own channel so nothing gets lost in one giant feed.

loglogslogging ignore
🎮

Games

17 games are built right in, from trivia and blackjack to hangman, connect 4, and snake. One command pulls up the whole menu.

games
